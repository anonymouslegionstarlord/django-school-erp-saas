How policy evaluation works

Each expense line is evaluated when saved. SpendPilot flags category-limit breaches, missing receipts above the configured threshold, and dates outside the report's trip window. Flags remain visible to the submitter and reviewer; they do not silently block legitimate exceptions. An approver must record a note before approving a flagged report.
